# FlavorBench reader’s guide

Choose a model on Home to open a documented example. Use the kitchen’s phase buttons or slider to inspect the scene. The written explanation, sources and step controls remain available if 3D fails.

Explore objects to read what each kitchen item represents. The Recipe book and Sources library distinguish developer disclosures from outside evidence. The six model releases are examples. Dishes and timings illustrate concepts; they do not represent measurements or model outputs.

Use Food recipes to browse curated dishes and adjust servings. The Cookbook saves items in your browser. Choose Generate a recipe with AI to make a new dish from pantry ingredients. Generation runs on your device and requires WebGPU. First use downloads about 300 MB. Review the method, quantities, and allergens before cooking.
