The takeaway

AI use varies by task and by product. Observed use is one kind of evidence about workplace change.

What supports it

Developer research based on its own product usage

Anthropic analyzed sampled Claude.ai conversations and first-party API transcripts from November 2025.

January 2026 Economic Index

The report distinguishes collaborative assistance from more automated use and examines task success and autonomy.

Methods and economic measures

What this does not establish

This is Claude usage, classified with models. It does not measure the whole economy or establish how many jobs were lost. Its January publication describes a specific earlier sample.

A question to take with you

Does the evidence measure usage, task performance, or employment?
