A kitchen for understanding AI.

FlavorBench uses familiar objects to make unfamiliar systems easier to inspect.

What you can learn here

Choose a model and trace its architecture, training data, infrastructure, compute, refinement, evaluation and outputs. Each explanation points to the material that supports it.

Model documentation explains how a system was made. Research and reporting help you ask what happens when people use it.

Explore an idea

Our explanation method

Start with a plain definition. Connect it to a named model release. Explain why it matters, show the evidence, and state where the analogy stops.

The cooking sequence is a teaching aid. Real teams test and revise throughout development. Food pairings and their cuisines are editorial choices.

Our editorial standards

Developer reports are labeled as developer reports. Independent reporting is labeled separately. Every factual model note cites its release documentation.

We keep undisclosed figures unknown. Company-wide investment is separate from model training costs. Publication dates and review dates describe different events.

Inspiration and limits

MIT’s ImpactBench inspired the path from overview to exploration to evidence. FlavorBench is an independent educational project with no MIT affiliation.

We do not assign wellbeing ratings, run benchmarks, train models, or rank these releases. Technical disclosures and observed use need context before drawing conclusions about human impact.

A living, curated collection

The six model recipes describe specified releases, including historical examples. Articles are curated rather than automatically ingested. Reviewed 2026-10-08.
