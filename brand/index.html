Identity

FlavorBench connects a familiar cooking process to the research, data and decisions behind an AI model. Our bowl mark, one-word name and green accent identify the kitchen.

Use one typeface and one baseline for the wordmark. Keep at least one mark-height of space around standalone logos. The minimum screen width is 140px. Use a white lockup on dark surfaces. Keep the proportions intact.

Visual reference: Uber Eats, inspected October 2026. FlavorBench uses its own mark, licensed typeface and original images.

Typography

Manrope is our self-hosted, open-source geometric sans-serif. One family connects exploration, model recipes and source explanations. Use sentence case, strong headings and readable explanations.

Imagery

One recognizable dish per menu image. Consistent angles, natural light, visible food texture and simple tableware. These images are original AI-generated illustrations; they do not show tested portions or model-generated recipes.

The interactive 3D scene connects objects to written notes. Objects represent concepts, never measured cost, dataset size or model quality. Every selectable object has an equivalent labeled control.

Interface

Align content to a consistent grid. Give food images room and keep the next action visible. Use flat surfaces, 8px action corners and 12px image frames. Keep the model dossiers quiet and preserve the food notebook’s binding.

Explore the kitchenDownload guideSelected recipe

Educational model dossiers use warm paper and a quiet notebook outline, with Inputs, Method, Outputs and Evidence anchors. The secondary food experience retains its bound notebook and green selected tabs. On phones, Menu opens navigation; the Kitchen stacks scene, objects and explanation. Pantry settings start collapsed. Written controls preserve every explanation when 3D is unavailable.

Voice

  • Use concrete verbs: choose, explore, read, cook, save.
  • Explain what went in, what happened and what came out.
  • Name the model release and link to its documentation.
  • Distinguish a source statement, an editorial pairing and a visual metaphor.
  • Show missing details as unknown. Never invent ratings, endorsements or a live connection.
  • Keep cooking methods, quantities, source credits and adaptation notes attached to each recipe.

The kitchen

The egg represents Transformer research. Grains represent training data. The stove represents infrastructure and investment. Heat represents compute, seasoning represents post-training and tasting represents evaluation. The dish represents a model; a serving represents its response.

The cooking metaphor makes the process easier to inspect. Evidence supports claims about resources, capabilities and impact. A cuisine does not describe a model’s ability.
